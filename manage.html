Registration close hote hi naam add kar do (time khaali chhod ke). Jaise-jaise session khatam ho, us row mein time daal ke phir se "Save" dabao.

Jab tak round ke SABHI participants ka time nahi bhara, tab tak top 8 (round ka naam "Final" ho to top 3) live page par halka yellow dikhenge — matlab "next round mein ja rahe hain (provisional)". Jaise hi sabka time bhar doge, wahi top 8/3 green ho jayenge — confirm advancing.

Push Final Results

Pehle upar se "Save Live Results" zaroor dabao — ye button Firestore mein jo abhi saved hai wahi se padhta hai.

Har event ke liye: agar koi round ka naam Final/Finals hai to wahi use hoga; agar us event ka sirf ek hi round hai to wahi use hoga (jab uske sab participants ka time bhar jaye); agar multiple rounds hain aur koi "Final" naam ka nahi to wo event skip ho jayega (summary mein dikh jayega).

Top 3 us round se seedha Manage Results table mein event row ban ke chala jayega (1st/2nd/3rd), aur us round ke SAARE valid time wale participants T&T Records (Top 10/Rankings) ki "Single" list mein bhi jud jayenge — naya record ban jaye to record page khud-ba-khud update ho jayega, aur live page par wahi time 🔥 TTR tag ke saath highlight hoga.
